### Added

- First published Battery Life AppImage for KDE Plasma on Wayland.
- A tray device list and an optional details window opened with `--window`.
- Battery checks every 30 seconds through Linux, BlueZ, UPower, and supported USB receiver protocols.
- Device diagnostics and udev rules for receiver access.
- Automated AppImage builds and release publishing through GitHub Actions.

### Changed

- Devices that have not been detected are hidden from the list.

### Fixed

- Account for the 8K receiver responses reported by the ZAOPIN Z2 Max.
- Distinguish battery power, charging, and power-off states on the MCHOSE V9 PRO, and mark its readings as approximate.
- Show the time of the device's last response in its submenu.
